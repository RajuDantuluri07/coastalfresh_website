Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AquaRythu

Can I use it without internet?

Yes! AquaRythu is built to work 100% offline at the pond. You can log all data without internet, and it will automatically sync to the cloud when you're back online.

What's the difference between Basic and Pro?

Basic adds feed guidance and alerts. Pro adds advanced analytics like FCR tracking, pond comparison, survival estimation, and multi-user roles for your team.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. Your farm data is encrypted and backed up daily. Only you and people you authorize can access your data.

Do you support multiple languages?

Currently we support English and Telugu. Tamil support is coming soon!

How do I get started?

Simply click "Start Free Forever" and create your account. You can add your first pond and start logging within 5 minutes. No credit card required.
