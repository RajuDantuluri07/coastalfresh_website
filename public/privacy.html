Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how AquaBook Pro ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information Collection and Use

AquaBook Pro is designed as an offline-first application. The majority of the data you generate (farm logs, tank details, feed entries, expenses) is stored locally on your device via LocalStorage.

a. Location Data

We request access to your device's location to:

  • Provide local weather forecasts specific to your farm.
  • Allow you to pinpoint and save your farm's location on a map.

This location data is processed on your device and sent directly to third-party services (Open-Meteo and Google Maps) to fetch relevant data. We do not store your location history on our external servers.

b. Device Storage

We may request access to your device's storage to allow you to:

  • Upload lab reports (CSV or Text files) for analysis.
  • Export and import data backups (JSON files) to prevent data loss.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or your device location. Links to the privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Maps API
  • Open-Meteo

3. Data Security

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device. We strongly recommend using the "Backup Data" feature within the app regularly to save your data to a secure external location (like Google Drive or WhatsApp).

4. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
