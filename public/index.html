About Us

From Our Shores to Your Door

Coastal Fresh was born from a simple idea: everyone deserves to experience the true taste of the coast, no matter where they live. Founded by a team of seafood lovers with deep roots in the coastal regions of Andhra, we were tired of the compromises city-dwellers had to make—settling for frozen, old, or improperly handled seafood.

We decided to change that. By building a direct-to-consumer supply chain, we connect directly with trusted fishermen and our own farms along the coast. This allows us to source the freshest catch, process it hygienically within hours, and deliver it to your kitchen in Hyderabad the very next day.

Our mission is to redefine the seafood experience in Hyderabad by delivering unparalleled freshness, quality, and convenience. We are committed to sustainable sourcing, meticulous hygiene, and preserving the authentic flavors of coastal cuisine.
