Why Choose Coastal Fresh?

At Coastal Fresh, we deliver premium, fresh seafood sourced daily from the coastal waters of Andhra Pradesh and trusted farms. From fish, prawns and crabs to specialty catches — we bring the best of the coast to your kitchen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is your seafood sourced from?
We work directly with local coastal fishermen and certified farmers from Coastal Andhra Pradesh. Our seafood is wild‑caught or farm‑fresh within 24 hours of delivery, ensuring export‑quality freshness every time.
How quickly will I receive my order?
All orders placed before 12 PM IST are delivered next day. Most customers receive their seafood within 24–48 hours. You’ll also get real‑time tracking updates once your order is shipped.
Is your seafood sustainable?
Yes ✅. We only partner with fisheries that follow responsible harvesting practices. Our partners use by‑catch reduction methods and follow seasonal fishing guidelines to protect marine ecosystems.
How do you guarantee freshness during delivery?
Your order is packed in insulated boxes with ice gel packs to maintain the ideal temperature until it reaches your doorstep — keeping your seafood fresh, safe, and ready to cook.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI payments, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
Do you deliver across Hyderabad?
Yes. We deliver to all major areas across Hyderabad, including Gachibowli, Kondapur, Madhapur, Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Secunderabad, and many more.
Do you clean and cut seafood before delivery?
Absolutely. All seafood is hygienically cleaned, properly cut, and packed based on your preference — curry, fry, or grill. For prawns, we also provide cleaned & deveined options.
Do you handle bulk orders for restaurants and events?
Yes ✅. We specialize in bulk seafood orders for restaurants, hotels, corporate events, weddings, caterers, festivals, and special occasions.
