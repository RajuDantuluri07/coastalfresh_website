About Us

Our Story

Fresh seafood, straight from the coast to your kitchen. We started Coastal Fresh to end the compromise with frozen or mishandled seafood. By working directly with fishermen and our own coastal farms, we bring you the day’s catch — processed within hours and delivered to Hyderabad the very next day.

Our Mission

To make seafood in Hyderabad as fresh as it is by the shore. We promise unmatched freshness, hygienic handling, and convenience at your doorstep.
