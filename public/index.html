Why Choose Coastal Fresh?

At Coastal Fresh, we bridge heritage and innovation. With roots in generations of aquaculture and over 20+ years of farming experience, we’ve grown from tradition into a modern retail brand.

From responsibly sourced prawns, fish, and crabs to specialty coastal catches, every order is fresh, hygienic, and sustainably packed — ensuring authentic taste and top quality.

Our mission is to make fresh, premium seafood accessible to every home in Hyderabad — delivering trust, freshness, and care straight from the coast to your door.
